The sanctuary.

Denise crafted the studio as a quiet, private space: warm clay tones, calm light, one client at a time. No salon rush, no chemical haze — just unhurried care for your natural nails.

Parking and arrival details are confirmed with Denise when you book, so your visit runs smoothly from the moment you arrive.

Before you arrive

Deposit first: A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to confirm every booking. Payment is immediate. The deposit is forfeited if you cancel less than 24 hours before your appointment, or if you do not show up.

Live diary: real-time slots are on Setmore. Choose your date and treatment there, or use the manual reservation form and confirm with Denise on WhatsApp.

Pre-visit guidance

Frequently asked arrival questions.

Your slot is reserved for you, so arriving on time is all that is needed. If you are running late, message Denise on WhatsApp as soon as you know.

Parking details are confirmed with Denise when you book — message her on WhatsApp and she will advise you.

A 50% non-refundable deposit confirms your booking and is paid immediately by EFT. Bank details are shared on request via WhatsApp. The deposit is forfeited if you cancel less than 24 hours before your appointment or do not show up.

The studio is a private residential atelier, so please ask Denise on WhatsApp before your visit if you need to bring someone with you.

If you can, arrive with bare, unvarnished natural nails. If you have existing product on your nails, choose the Safe Removal & Aftercare treatment so Denise can remove it safely first.
